# Landing page images

Drop these files here (exact names expected by `LandingPage.tsx`). Until a file exists, the
landing page falls back to a gradient/icon placeholder, so the site works fine either way.

| Filename | Used for | Suggested subject |
|---|---|---|
| `hero-seafarer.jpg` | Hero section (background) | Southeast Asian seafarer/ship crew, warm and confident, not overly posed |
| `product-business.jpg` | Business Loan card | Southeast Asian small business owner (shop, market stall, small enterprise) |
| `product-salary.jpg` | Salary Loan card | Southeast Asian office worker in corporate attire |
| `product-seafarer.jpg` | Seafarer Loan card | Southeast Asian seafarer (deck, uniform, or with family before deployment) |

Recommended size: at least 1200px on the longest side, `.jpg` or `.webp`, licensed for commercial
use (e.g. Unsplash License - free, no attribution required). See the search links shared in chat
for starting points.
